## Lab 02 – Agile Backlog Creation and Sprint Simulation in Jira

Translating the Lab 01 requirements into an agile product backlog and simulating a sprint in Jira.

### Contents

* **[Agile Backlog Creation and Sprint Simulation in Jira](Lab-02/PES1UG24CS666-Agile%20backlog%20creation%20and%20sprint%20simulation%20in%20Jira.pdf)** – Jira screenshots of the product backlog, sprint board, and burndown chart, followed by the reflection questions.

### Reflection Topics

* Whether the story-point estimations reflected the actual effort.
* How well the backlog was prioritized — pet search, adoption applications, medical records, and application review as high priority, foster placement management as medium.
* How the simulated sprint aligned with the planned backlog.
* What the burndown chart showed about the team's capacity and sprint progress.
